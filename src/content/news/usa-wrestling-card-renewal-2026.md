All wrestlers participating in Midway practices need an active USA Wrestling Athlete Membership for the current membership year. USA Wrestling's 2026-27 membership year runs from September 1, 2026 through August 31, 2027.

Please complete your wrestler's renewal before attending practice. Keeping the membership current helps meet the insurance requirements for our wrestlers and the schools that allow us to use their facilities.

Renewal checklist:
- Visit [USAW Membership](https://www.usawmembership.com/)
- Sign in to your existing account
- Go to **Manage → Memberships** and choose **Purchase / Renew Membership**
- Select your wrestler's profile and complete checkout
- Confirm that the athlete membership is active for the 2026-27 season
- Review the athlete's profile and club affiliation for accuracy

If you have trouble renewing, message the [Midway Wrestling Club Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/share/184nobE311/?mibextid=wwXIfr) before the first practice.
