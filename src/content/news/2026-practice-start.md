Midway Wrestling Club practices begin Wednesday, October 7th. We are excited to get everyone back on the mat for the 2026 season.

Practice details:
- **Start date:** Wednesday, October 7th
- **Practice nights:** Mondays and Wednesdays
- **Time:** 6:45-8:00 PM
- **October location:** North Fork High School
- **November and December location:** Delta High School

There will be no practice on Wednesday, November 25 or Wednesday, December 23. Check the [season calendar](/schedule) for the complete schedule and any future updates.

Before attending, please make sure your wrestler has an active USA Wrestling Athlete Membership. Wrestling shoes and athletic clothing are required.

New to Midway? Message the [Midway Wrestling Club Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/share/184nobE311/?mibextid=wwXIfr) to confirm that our practices are the right fit and that space is available for your wrestler.
